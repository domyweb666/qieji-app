## 三、審查備註（英文，整格換掉）

```
Version 2.0 is a full rewrite of the app in SwiftUI (1.1 was built with Expo). The features are the same as 1.1. There is no new in-app purchase: the existing non-consumable "茄記完整版" (org.domyweb.qieji.full, Family Sharing enabled), approved with 1.1, is unchanged. Notes and purchases from 1.1 carry over on update.

No account or login is required. The app does not collect any data. It has no servers, analytics or ads. It does not request photo library, camera, microphone or location access: cover images are chosen with the system photo picker, and Face ID is used only when the user locks a note.

The purchase unlocks iCloud Drive sync, the Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets, the Control Center control "記一行" (iOS 18+), cover images, per-note lock with Face ID, a free-form notebook page, and a text size setting. Writing, crossing out, organizing and searching notes are free.

To test the purchase: open a note, tap "⋯" at the top right, then "設定". (The same page is also at the bottom of the directory: tap the list name at the top left, "管理所有清單", then "設定".) Tap "茄記完整版" to open the purchase sheet. "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) is on the same settings page and on the purchase sheet.

Before purchase, the widgets show a locked message and the Control Center control opens the purchase sheet instead of the input field. The paid entries are hidden until purchase: "放封面圖" and "上鎖" in the "⋯" menu, "新的記事本" in the list switcher, and "紙上的字級" in settings. The "iCloud 同步" row in settings shows a lock and opens the purchase sheet. Not having the purchase never deletes anything: notes stay on the device and existing files stay in the user's iCloud Drive. If a purchase is refunded, existing cover images, notebook pages and locked notes remain usable.

To test the paid features after purchase: open a note, tap "⋯" at the top right for "放封面圖" (cover image) and "上鎖" (lock); tap the list name at the top left for "新的記事本" (new notebook); text size is in "設定 › 紙上的字級".

Purchases use StoreKit 2 only. No third-party SDK or server is involved.

The settings page has a few links that open outside the app, in Safari, Mail or the App Store: feedback by email, my website, my Facebook and Instagram pages, the privacy policy, the App Store review page, and the App Store page of my other app "豆鍵" (under "蔬菜家族的其他 app"). The app itself makes no network requests other than iCloud Drive sync and StoreKit.
```
