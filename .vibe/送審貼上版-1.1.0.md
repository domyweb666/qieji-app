## 二、App 審查資訊 › 備註（英文，整格換掉）

```
No account or login is required. The app does not collect any data. It has no servers, analytics or ads. It does not request photo library, camera, microphone or location access: cover images are chosen with the system photo picker, and Face ID is used only when the user locks a note.

Version 1.1 adds one non-consumable in-app purchase, "茄記完整版" (org.domyweb.qieji.full), with Family Sharing enabled. It unlocks iCloud Drive sync, the Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets, the Control Center control "記一行" (iOS 18+), cover images, per-note lock with Face ID, a free-form notebook page, and a text size setting. Writing, crossing out, organizing and searching notes remain free.

To test the purchase: tap the list name at the top left, tap "管理所有清單" at the bottom, then "設定" at the bottom of the directory. Tap "茄記完整版" to open the purchase sheet. "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) is on the same settings page and on the purchase sheet.

Before purchase, the widgets show a locked message and the Control Center control opens the purchase sheet instead of the input field. Not having the purchase never deletes anything: notes stay on the device and existing files stay in the user's iCloud Drive. If a purchase is refunded, existing cover images, notebook pages and locked notes remain usable.

To test the paid features after purchase: open a note, tap "⋯" at the top right for "放封面圖" (cover image) and "上鎖" (lock); tap the list name at the top left for "新的記事本" (new notebook); text size is in "設定 › 紙上的字級".

Users who had iCloud sync in version 1.0 see a one-time notice after updating. Their notes and iCloud files are untouched; sync resumes after purchase.

Purchases use StoreKit 2 only. No third-party purchase SDK or server is involved.
```
